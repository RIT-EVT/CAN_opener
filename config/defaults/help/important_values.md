# Important Signals

These are the signals your team has marked as important for this vehicle platform. The list is saved with the platform, so every log opened on that platform shows the same list, and PDF reports include these signals.

- **Click a signal** in either list to plot it. Hold Ctrl or Shift to pick several at once.
- **Add a signal** by dragging it from **All signals** up into **Important signals**. Drop it where you want it in the list. You can also right-click a signal and choose **Add to important signals**.
- **Reorder** by dragging signals up and down inside the Important signals list.
- **Remove a signal** by dragging it back down to All signals, selecting it and pressing Delete, or right-clicking it.
- **Plot by dragging** any signal straight onto the graph.
- **Filter** both lists at once with the search box above them.
- The **pencil button** opens a checklist of every signal, which is quicker for large changes.
- A signal shown dimmed is on the platform's list but isn't in this log. It stays on the list for other logs.
- In **All signals**, a star marks the signals that are already important.
- Signals with a DBC value table (on/off states, fault codes and similar) are plotted as coded numbers, and their state names appear at the cursors.
